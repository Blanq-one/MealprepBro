## Held items with versions (J4)

**pepperoni**: Which pepperoni is it? Check the label: read the ingredients list. Pork and beef (or beef fat) means it's off your list. Pork only counts toward your pork limit. Turkey or chicken with no pork or beef is fine. If the label doesn't say, choose Not sure.

- Pork and beef: pepperoni-pork-beef
- Pork only: pepperoni-pork (nutrition estimated)
- Turkey: pepperoni-turkey
- Chicken: pepperoni-chicken (nutrition estimated)
- Not sure: stays the held item

**salami**: Which salami is it? Check the label: read the ingredients list. Pork and beef (or beef fat) means it's off your list. Pork only counts toward your pork limit. Turkey or chicken with no pork or beef is fine. If the label doesn't say, choose Not sure.

- Pork and beef: salami-pork-beef
- Pork only: salami-pork
- Turkey: salami-turkey
- Chicken: salami-chicken (nutrition estimated)
- Not sure: stays the held item

**chicken-bouillon-cube**: Which chicken bouillon is it (cubes or powder)? Check the label: look for beef, beef fat, beef extract or tallow in the ingredients. If none is listed, it's fine. If the label is unclear or missing, choose Not sure.

- Chicken, no beef listed: chicken-bouillon-no-beef
- Contains beef or beef fat: chicken-bouillon-beef
- Not sure: stays the held item
